# StudyBuddy – Product Assumptions

## 1. Product Overview

StudyBuddy is a student-friendly productivity platform designed to help students manage their academic activities easily in one place. It allows students to organize daily study tasks, manage assignments, create study schedules, and monitor their learning progress. The main goal of StudyBuddy is to make students' academic life more organized, productive, and stress-free.


## 2. Problem Statement

Students often find it difficult to manage their study tasks, assignments, and daily schedules. They may forget important work or struggle to organize their time. StudyBuddy aims to solve this problem by providing a simple platform to manage academic activities in one place.


## 3. Target Users

StudyBuddy is mainly designed for school and college students who want to organize their studies, manage daily tasks, and improve their productivity.

## 4. Main Use Case

Students can use StudyBuddy to plan their daily study activities, add and manage tasks, set deadlines for assignments, organize their study schedules, and track completed and pending work. They can access their academic activities from one dashboard and stay organized throughout their studies.

## 5. Core Features

1. **Dashboard:** Displays an overview of daily tasks, study hours, and progress.
2. **Task Management:** Allows students to add, edit, delete, and complete study tasks.
3. **Timetable**: Helps students create and manage their daily class timetables.
4. **Progress Tracking:** Shows completed and pending tasks to help students monitor their progress.

## 6. User Assumptions

- Students need a simple platform to manage their academic activities.
- Students prefer managing all their study tasks in one place.
- Students want to track their daily study progress.
- Students prefer an easy-to-use interface without complicated features.

## 7. MVP Scope

The first version of StudyBuddy will focus on providing essential features that help students organize their studies.

The MVP will include:

1. Student dashboard.
2. Add, edit, and delete study tasks.
3. Mark tasks as completed.
4. Create and manage study schedules.
5. View completed and pending tasks.
6. Store data using browser localStorage.

## 8. Out of Scope

The following features will not be included in the initial version of StudyBuddy:

1. AI-powered study recommendations.
2. Online group study and chat.
3. Video calling and live classes.
4. Payment and subscription features.
5. Complex analytics and performance reports.
