1. Project Idea
Study Buddy is a study planner tool that will be easy to use for students to structure and plan subjects, to create study tasks, and to monitor and control the learning.
Study Buddy aims to be a tool to keep students organised while studying.
2. Problem Statement
Students faced with coursework can struggle to effectively organize subjects make study schedules finish work and keep the unit syllabus up to date.
A lot of students have notebooks where they set reminders or use apps to organise their study but it can all get a little messy.
Study Buddy provides a simple and easy to use interface to students to manage their study stuff.
3. Target Users
* School students
* College students
* Engineering students
* Students for exam preparation
* People who wish to learn how to study better.
4. Primary Use Case
The Student leverages Study Buddy to handle their study tasks.
They can make subjects, plan tasks, choose when they want to study and complete mark tasks. Record how they are progressing overall.
Here is an illustration, a college scholar has added to topics such as Mathematics, Operating Systems, Data Structures. They may create duty within each class. See which topics they have finished.
5. Key Features
•Subject management
• Organize your daily/weekly study plan
• Task management
• Mark activities done and monitor your learning progress
To do list
Basic dashboard listing subjects, study work and completed tasks.
Easy to use interface.
6. User Assumptions
We are assuming that students will want to:
A simple way to plan their study schedule.
Manage subjects in one place.
Use an easy to use interface.
Keep track of what they have done and what they still have to do.
Bite-sized sessions can make it easy to stay on track.
Happy to use a tool to improve their study behaviours.
We will check this by asking students how they study, what they do to prepare for studying and whether they have any problems.
7. Problem Validation
Before we even start to build the product we need to know whether students really have these problems or not.
We shall verify this through:
Inquire about study skill practices used by students.
What issues they have with managing subjects.
How they track what they have completed.
Asking for feedback on their study planning.
What features would help them the most.
This will help us make our idea better and will know what features we should work on.
8. MVP
Our MVP will include all core features that will help solve our problem.
Features include:
Adding and managing subjects.
Creating study tasks.
Organising tasks into subjects.
Knowing what you are supposed to study that day.
Marking tasks as complete.
Knowing what you are supposed to do and what you've already finished.
Basic progress tracker.
Easy to use interface.
9. Features Not Included
• AI suggestions for study
• Online video lessons
• Working together of students
• Social media
• Performance analytics
• Automatic syllabus creation
• Learning platform connectivity
• Advanced notifications/reminders
• These could be considered for future versions.
10. Product Assumption
We think that Study Buddy will be helpful to students to keep them organised with how they manage their subjects planning their study doing their tasks and keeping track of their learning.
A study planner that is clean and simple to use will be helpful to the students.
This we will validate by getting feedback from students and using our MVP.
11. Inspiration
Inspired by Product Hunt.
The idea we got from the study planners on Product Hunt.
We will develop our variant of Study Buddy using our proprietary design, features and experience.
We will not copy any existing products.
12. Conclusion
Study Buddy is designed to make studying simple and well organised.
Our initial design will enable students to plan which subjects they will take manage study tasks and monitor their progress.
We will develop the MVP and receive feedback from students so that we can validate our product assumptions and improve Study Buddy.
